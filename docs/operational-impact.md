# Operational Impact on Frozen Test Predictions

Generated by `scripts/operational_impact.py`; do not edit by hand. Numbers are in `docs/operational_impact_results.json`.

## What this measures

How the frozen routing policy would distribute reviewer attention across 1,845 sessions, and where the 286 converting sessions (base rate 15.5%) would land. It answers an operations question (how much review work, and how many conversions sit in the queue versus the automatic log), not a revenue question.

## Workload and coverage

- Automatically logged: 32.4% of sessions need no reviewer time.
- Review queue (priority + human review + fallback): 67.6% of sessions, i.e. 675.9 per 1,000 sessions (479.7 priority, 196.2 human review).
- That queue contains 95.5% of all converting sessions. A random selection of the same size would be expected to contain 67.6%.
- 13 converting sessions (4.5%) end up in the automatic log: this is the cost of not reviewing low-scored sessions.
- Policy consistency: 362 of 362 uncertain sessions were routed to human review; 0 safety fallbacks occurred.

## How to read this

- The threshold was chosen on validation data with a 5:1 false-negative to false-positive cost ratio, so the policy deliberately sends many sessions to review in order to miss few conversions. The queue is large by design; a team with less capacity would need a different, separately validated threshold.
- "Route conversion rate" is the share of sessions in that route that converted in the dataset. 25.2% of priority-review sessions converted, against a base rate of 15.5%: priority means "look first", not "will buy".

## Non-claims and limitations

- No revenue, profit, uplift, or ROI is estimated.
- Routes reflect the written policy as reproduced by the deterministic provider. A live language model could choose a different route for a clear-cut session; the validator still forces every uncertain session to human review.
- Public dataset, offline analysis; not connected to a live event stream.
- Test numbers are reported once, from frozen predictions; they were not used to choose the model, threshold, band, or policy.
